Services & Programs

Whether you're looking to manage a medical condition, achieve a healthy weight, or fuel your athletic performance, our evidence-based services are tailored to your unique biology.

Our Core Specialties

Weight Management

Sustainable fat loss and muscle gain strategies without restrictive dieting. Learn portion control, healthy swaps, and mindful eating habits.

  • Custom macro targets
  • Craving management

Clinical Nutrition

Medical nutrition therapy for managing Diabetes, PCOS, Hypertension, Thyroid issues, and high cholesterol through dietary intervention.

  • Bloodwork analysis
  • Medication interactions

Sports Performance

Fueling protocols for athletes and active individuals. Optimize your pre-workout energy, intra-workout stamina, and post-workout recovery.

  • Carb-cycling plans
  • Supplement guidance

Gut & Microbiome Health

Relief from bloating, IBS, and digestive discomfort. We utilize elimination diets and low-FODMAP protocols to identify your unique triggers.

  • Food sensitivity tracking
  • Probiotic optimization

Pre & Postnatal Diet

Ensuring optimal nourishment for both mother and baby during pregnancy, and supporting healthy recovery and lactation postpartum.

  • Trimester-specific needs
  • Safe postpartum weight loss

Corporate Wellness

Group seminars, team challenges, and executive health programs designed to boost workplace productivity and reduce absenteeism.

  • Lunch & learn webinars
  • Team dashboard access

The Consultation Process

A structured, 4-step framework to ensure you get the results you deserve.

1

Discovery Call

A 15-minute free intro session to discuss your goals, struggles, and determine the best program for you.

2

Deep Assessment

A comprehensive 60-minute consultation analyzing your medical history, labs, and dietary preferences.

3

Plan Delivery

Receive your custom meal plan and dashboard access within 48 hours, fully tailored to your lifestyle.

4

Track & Adjust

Bi-weekly follow-ups to review dashboard data, overcome plateaus, and adjust macros as you progress.
