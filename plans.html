Pricing FAQ

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time directly through your dashboard settings. Prorated charges will apply.

Do you accept health insurance?

While we don't bill insurance directly, we provide super-bills upon request for our Clinical Care plan which you can submit for reimbursement.

Is there a cancellation fee?

No hidden fees. You can cancel your monthly subscription anytime before your next billing cycle without any penalties.

How does the dashboard work?

Once subscribed, you'll receive login credentials. The web-based dashboard works on any device and houses your meals, tracking graphs, and chat.
