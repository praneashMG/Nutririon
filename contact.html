Common Questions

Quick answers to help you get the support you need faster.

How fast do you respond?

Our client success team typically responds to email inquiries within 24 hours during standard business days. For urgent dashboard issues, please use the in-app chat.

How do I reschedule a call?

You can reschedule your consultation directly from the "Appointments" tab in your client dashboard up to 24 hours before the scheduled time.

Do you work with healthcare providers?

Yes. If you need us to coordinate your dietary plan with your primary care physician or specialist, please select the 'Clinical Care' plan and notify our team.

I can't access my dashboard

If you're having trouble logging in, please click "Forgot Password" on the login screen. If the issue persists, email support@wellora.com with your account email.
