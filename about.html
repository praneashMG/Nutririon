Our Story
How It Started

Born from a Desire to Change the System

WELLORA was founded in 2021 by a group of clinical dietitians and software engineers who were tired of seeing people fall victim to dangerous fad diets and one-size-fits-all meal plans.

We realized the biggest gap in nutritional care wasn't a lack of information, but a lack of personalization and execution. Traditional consultations gave clients a PDF and sent them on their way. We knew there had to be a better way to support long-term behavioral change.

By integrating world-class dietary expertise with our proprietary client dashboard, we created a seamless ecosystem where experts can guide clients daily, rather than just once a month.

Our Core Philosophy

The guiding principles that dictate every meal plan we write and every feature we build.

Science First

We strictly adhere to peer-reviewed, evidence-based nutritional science. No detox teas, no magic pills.

Sustainability

The best diet is the one you can stick to. We focus on building lifelong habits, not temporary restrictions.

Empathy

We understand that food is deeply personal and emotional. We offer a judgment-free, highly supportive environment.

Innovation

By merging clinical expertise with modern technology, we make tracking, logging, and succeeding effortless.
